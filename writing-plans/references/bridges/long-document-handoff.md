# Long-Document Handoff

## Purpose

Transfer a large-source planning document to Long Document Segmented Writing without duplicating its reading, scratch, drafting, or confidence-repair workflow.

## Inputs

- Settled document goal, audience, scope, non-goals, and final path.
- Required source files and exact ranges already known.
- Required sections, locked decisions, terminology, evidence rules, and unresolved blockers.

## Required result

Provide one compact handoff containing the inputs above and explicitly invoke `$long-document-segmented-writing`. Point to authoritative sources; do not paste large excerpts. The long-document owner chooses its scratch, ledger, segmented drafting, reread, and whole-draft review mechanics.

## Failure boundary

Return unresolved product intent, root cause, architecture, or source authority to Software Quality Workflows before transfer. After transfer, load no other Writing Plans reference and create no parallel outline, scratch tree, capsule, or sidecar.
