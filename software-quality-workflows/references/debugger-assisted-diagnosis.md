# Debugger-Assisted Diagnosis

> Owner: debugger-assisted-diagnosis
> Authority: companion
> Role: recipe
> Phases: DIAGNOSING
> Requires: systematic-debugging, verification-discipline
> May load: none
> Does not own: root-cause verdict, change authority, implementation

Use only after a reproducible failure and falsifiable hypothesis identify a debugger as the cheapest discriminating observation. Bind breakpoints/watchpoints and captured state to source revision, redact sensitive values, and convert the result into a replayable reproduction or explicit limitation.
